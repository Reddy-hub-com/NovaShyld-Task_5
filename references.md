## Primary Tools

### Nmap

Used for network reconnaissance, port discovery, service
enumeration, version detection, and vulnerability assessment.

### Metasploit Framework

Used for controlled vulnerability validation and exploitation
against the authorized Metasploitable2 laboratory target.

### Meterpreter

Used for controlled post-exploitation validation after successful
exploitation.
